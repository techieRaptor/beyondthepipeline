# The hardest problems are rarely syntax

Frameworks change quickly. The durable skill is understanding the system around the code.

## Business parity beats implementation parity

Two implementations can look completely different and still be equivalent if they preserve the same business behavior.

## Observability is part of the design

A pipeline without useful operational signals is difficult to trust. Build for diagnosis, not just execution.

## Architecture is a trade-off

There is rarely one perfect design. The best architecture is the one whose operational cost and complexity match the problem.
